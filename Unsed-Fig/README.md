# Unused figures from the seven source papers

This folder originally collected copies of the 22 active, numbered figures that were identified as completely unused in the thesis. Multi-panel figures are represented by their individual source assets, giving 29 image/source files in total. The source-paper files were not moved or modified.

Eight of these figures have since been integrated into the thesis and copied to the `Fig` folder: BCD-ESC F07, BCD-MPC/AIM F07, CO2 F05, CO2 F06, oil-spill F03, RSSI F01, RSSI F02, and swarm/MECC F03. The folder therefore remains a historical source collection; 14 of its numbered figures remain unused in the thesis.

The partially reused three-panel ESC-method figure from the BCD-ESC paper is not included because one of its panels is already used in the thesis.
